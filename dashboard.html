01 / Permission
Review required

Provider-approved assignment

No provider workflow connected
02 / Payments
Approval required

Payments need partner approval

SeatSwap does not collect, hold, or route money. A permitted payment and refund process must be agreed with a payment provider before launch.

No payment integration
03 / Support
Workflow undefined

Access and support need a plan

Providers may not expose a reliable way to monitor an assigned seat. A pilot needs a support, cancellation, and refund process that does not promise unavailable automation.
